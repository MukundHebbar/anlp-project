## Prospective manual-boundary clarification

Added after AH/AJ completed, before inspecting any AK question content or making any AK model call. AH80/137 passed all automatic checks but were manually excluded for near-complete-method uncertainty. That is not demonstrated direct question leakage, and the user's request explicitly permits increasing information while withholding the whole question/derivation. Preserve those historical exclusions; do not rescore them as successes.

For AK, familiarity or visibility of the method alone is not a manual veto. Require an essential original premise to remain undisclosed, at least one actual calculation needed to obtain the forward answer relation to remain unstated, and an inverse inference or constrained choice to recover that premise. A named theorem plus setup may pass. Reject a full equivalent question, a disclosed missing value disguised as arithmetic, or a completed parameterized answer relation with the entire forward derivation already supplied. The publicly supplied final answer is intentionally available. Record the precise missing calculation, premise and inverse inference; uncertainty about any of these remains unresolved rather than silently accepted.

The model audit prompts are unchanged and all their required acceptances remain necessary. This clarifies the additional manual method-exposure gate, not a license to override rejected audits or accept incorrect clues. Private inverse certificates still need independent checking. AK therefore tests a prospectively clarified review package on a fresh development cohort; it is not a pure replication isolating dataset effects. This change must be included in any paper comparison, and cannot retroactively improve AH/AF/AG denominators or outcomes.
