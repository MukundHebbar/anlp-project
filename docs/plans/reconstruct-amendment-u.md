# Amendment U: distinguish reconstruction from restating a supplied claim

Prospective calibration, registered after S and T, before changing the evaluator or collecting new calibration judgments. Existing evaluate_p2 scores remain immutable. This amendment proposes a candidate criterion; it is not adopted merely by being written.

Observed failure mode: the public observations for source33 state the dominance/maxima lemma. Both Qwen and the stronger model turn that lemma into a yes/no question. The current evaluator gives scores3 or4 when their verification is adequate. These are mathematically valid questions, but their essential content is already supplied. They exercise restatement/judgment rather than the intended inference of missing question structure. Copy review already distinguishes them from recovery of the original equal-maxima theorem, so they never count toward5/25; the remaining issue is the grounding reward itself.

Candidate refinement: a score3/4 reconstruction must infer or construct an essential premise, mathematical target, parameter or relation absent from the combined public input. Merely converting a stated assertion into a question is a copy-only shortcut and scores0, even if the supplied assertion has a valid proof. Cosmetic changes or irrelevant added premises cannot manufacture substantive reconstruction. Valid alternatives remain eligible if they require actual missing-content construction and meet the existing correctness/grounding requirements. This does not turn source similarity into the sole reward.

Use a compact evaluator candidate derived from p2 with one global rule, an explicit substantive/copy-only/uncertain assessment, and at most two small examples. Keep all other score meanings fixed. Do not introduce source-specific instructions about maxima, radicals, or the failed Qwen outputs. Verify that the new field cannot claim substantive inference while the score/shortcut fields contradict it.

Before calls, author and mathematically check a fixed development manifest with at least four families not used in the new prompt examples. Include paired true-lemma copies versus genuinely missing-content reconstructions, decorative additions to copies, valid alternatives, and ordinary incorrect answers. Record exact expected score/shortcut labels and derivations before scoring. Also retain the observed source33 and scale-point copies as production diagnostics, separated from authored-control accuracy. Use identical cases for p2 and the candidate.

Progression: all clear copying controls rejected, all independently verified substantive-positive controls accepted at their intended score, and no unexplained new regressions on wrong-answer controls. Disagreements require case review and remain visible; do not rewrite reference labels just to obtain agreement. These small development checks cannot establish98% population accuracy or the final independent validation claim.

Compare score changes and whether the correction removes the known restatement reward. An apparent shift into or out of20–75 is a consequence to report, not a reason to change the rubric. A stricter rubric does not itself improve Qwen's zero semantic recovery.
